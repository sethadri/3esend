Getting Started
Click "Get Started" on the homepage and follow the 3-step sign-up. You'll need a valid email, phone number, and government-issued ID for verification. The whole process takes under 5 minutes.
We accept a valid passport, national ID card, or driving licence. You'll also need to take a quick selfie for identity confirmation. All documents are handled securely and never shared.
Most verifications are instant. In some cases it can take up to 1 business day if manual review is required. You'll be notified by email as soon as you're verified.
Sending Money
Most transfers arrive within minutes. Mobile wallet deposits are typically instant. Bank transfers can take 1–2 business days depending on the destination country and receiving bank.
You can cancel a transfer from your dashboard as long as it hasn't been processed yet. Once funds are in transit, cancellation may not be possible. Contact our support team immediately if you need to cancel.
Starter accounts can send up to £500 per transfer. Pro accounts go up to £5,000. Business accounts have custom limits. Higher personal limits are available with enhanced verification.
Rates & Fees
We use the mid-market exchange rate, updated hourly, with a transparent small margin that's disclosed before you send. We never hide fees in the rate — what you see is what your recipient gets.
Absolutely not. The fee shown at checkout — typically £1.99 to £2.99 — is the only fee you pay. No bank receiving fees, no mystery charges.
