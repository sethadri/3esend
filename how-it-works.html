How 3E Send Works

Sending money internationally shouldn't be complex. We've made it as simple as 3 steps.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email address and complete our quick, secure identity verification. It takes under 5 minutes and you only do it once. We're fully regulated, so your details are handled with care.

2

Add your recipient

Enter your recipient's name and choose how they'd like to receive the money — bank transfer, mobile wallet, or cash pickup. Save them as a contact for faster future transfers.

3

Choose amount & currency

Enter how much you want to send. Our calculator shows the exact exchange rate and fees upfront — no surprises. Lock in the rate and proceed with confidence.

4

Pay securely

Pay by debit card, credit card, or bank transfer. All payments are processed through secure, encrypted channels. You'll receive an instant confirmation once your payment is processed.

5

Money delivered

Your recipient gets notified and receives the funds, usually within minutes. You can track every step in real time from your dashboard, and we'll send you updates along the way.
